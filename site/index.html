Questions

What permission does it need, and why?
Accessibility. macOS asks the first time you open Jumpr. It’s how Jumpr finds the buttons, links and icons on your screens, and how it clicks the one you pick. Jumpr does not record your screen.
Does anything leave my Mac?
What Jumpr reads to place labels stays on your Mac. Its one request is a daily check for a new version, which you can turn off from Jumpr’s menu bar icon. The privacy policy has the details.
Is it really free?
Yes. Jumpr is free and open source under the MIT license, with no account and nothing to unlock. The code is on GitHub. Found a bug? Open an issue.
What do I need?
A Mac running macOS 15 or later. Jumpr works with one display and shines with two or more.
How do updates work?
Jumpr checks once a day and tells you when a new version is ready, then installs it when you say so.
Can I change the hotkey?
Yes, from Jumpr’s menu bar icon. The default is ⌘⇧Space.
