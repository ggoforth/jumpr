Using Jumpr

Labels, clicks, scrolling and settings, one page each.

Jumpr does two things from the keyboard: it clicks what you pick with a label, and it scrolls the area you pick in scroll mode. These pages cover both, and the panel where you change how they work.

Labels and keysPress the hotkey, type a label, and Jumpr clicks it.Kinds of clickHold a key on the last letter of a label to right-click, double-click and more.Persistent modeKeep the labels up for a run of clicks, such as opening a site, then Play, then Start.ScrollingScroll any area on any display from the keyboard, without moving the pointer.The panelClick the menu bar icon to pause Jumpr, preview your labels and change its settings.
