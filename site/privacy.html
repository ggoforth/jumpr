Privacy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

The short version

Jumpr works on your Mac. What it reads to place labels never leaves your Mac. The app has no account and no analytics. Its only network request is a daily check for updates, which you can turn off.

What Jumpr reads, and why

To put a label on each clickable thing, Jumpr uses the macOS Accessibility permission to find buttons, links, icons and menus on your displays, and to click the one you choose. This happens on your Mac. None of it is sent anywhere.

The update check

Once a day, Jumpr asks https://getjumpr.app/appcast.xml whether a new version is out. Like any web request, it shows our server your IP address, along with the Jumpr version and macOS version. We use that only to offer the right update. You can turn the check off in Jumpr’s Settings, and check by hand from the menu.

Your license key

Jumpr checks your key on your Mac, without going online, and keeps it in ~/Library/Application Support/Jumpr/.

When you buy

  • Payment is handled by Stripe. We receive your email address and the purchase record. We never see your full card number.
  • Your key is emailed to you through Resend, which keeps a delivery record.
  • We use your email address to send your key, answer you, and handle refunds. Nothing else. We don’t sell it or share it.

This website

No cookies, no analytics, no tracking, and no scripts from anyone else. The demo on the home page runs entirely in your browser. Our hosting provider may keep standard access logs, such as IP address and the page requested.
