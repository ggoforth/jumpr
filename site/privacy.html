Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Jumpr is a fully local Mac app. What it reads on your screen stays on your Mac, and there is no account to create. A few things do leave your Mac, each one because you asked for something that needs it. This page lists every one of them: what is sent, to whom, why, and what you can ask us to do about it.

1. Who is responsible

The controller of the personal data described here is BaggaDonuts LLC, a California limited liability company, 1187 N Willow Ave #103-815, Clovis, CA 93611, United States, trading as Jumpr. Reach us at support@getjumpr.app. We have not appointed a data protection officer; the same address reaches the person responsible for privacy.

2. The short version

  • The app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no telemetry and no crash reporting.
  • What Jumpr reads to place its labels never leaves your Mac. It is not recorded, and it is not stored or sent anywhere.
  • The app’s only network request is a daily check for updates, which you can turn off.
  • When you buy, Stripe takes the payment and we keep its record of the purchase so we can send your key and handle refunds.
  • You can ask us to show, correct or delete what we hold. Section 9 says how.

3. What stays on your Mac

  • Everything Jumpr reads through the macOS Accessibility permission: the buttons, links, icons and menus on your displays, and where they are. Jumpr uses it to place labels and to click the one you choose.
  • What you type while labels are showing.
  • Your settings (label size and colours, hotkey, switches), kept in the app’s preferences at ~/Library/Preferences/com.greggoforth.jumpr.plist.
  • Your license key and your trial start date, kept in ~/Library/Application Support/Jumpr/. The trial start date is also kept in the preferences.
  • A small log of Jumpr’s own activity, at ~/Library/Logs/Jumpr.log: when it showed labels, how long that took, and for each press the kind of control and the name of the app it was in. It does not log window contents, text on screen, or what you type. The log stays on your Mac and is capped in size.

Your license key is checked on your Mac, offline. Removing the app, the preferences file, the Jumpr folder in Application Support and the log removes everything related to Jumpr from your Mac.

4. What leaves your Mac, and why

Each item below is one thing that leaves your Mac. “Basis” is the legal ground we rely on under the GDPR and similar laws: contract means we need it to provide something you asked for; legitimate interest means we need it to run the service in a way you would reasonably expect.

Update checks

What is sent. Once a day, a request to getjumpr.app asking whether a new version is out. Like any web request, it carries your IP address and a user agent naming Jumpr and its version.

Why, and basis. To offer you the right update. Legitimate interest. You can turn the check off in Jumpr’s Settings.

Kept. In our hosting provider’s ordinary access logs.

Downloading an update

What is sent. When you choose to install an update, Jumpr downloads it from getjumpr.app. The request carries the same information as the update check.

Why, and basis. To deliver the update you asked for. Contract.

Kept. In our hosting provider’s ordinary access logs.

Payments

What is sent. Checkout happens on Stripe, which collects your card details. We never see them. We receive your email address, what you bought, the amount and the date.

Why, and basis. To deliver your license key, handle refunds and keep accounting records. Contract; records are also a legal obligation.

Kept. Your purchase record stays in Stripe for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

Your license key email

What is sent. After you pay, your license key is emailed to the address you gave at checkout, through Resend, which processes the address and the message. The key itself contains that email address.

Why, and basis. To get you your key. Contract.

Kept. Resend keeps delivery records under its own policy.

Email you send us

What is sent. Whatever you write to support@getjumpr.app, and your address.

Why, and basis. To read and answer you. Contract, or legitimate interest where there is no purchase.

This website

What is sent. Nothing beyond what your browser sends to load a page. The site has no analytics, sets no cookies of its own, and runs no trackers or scripts from anyone else. The demo on the home page runs entirely in your browser.

Kept. Amazon Web Services, which hosts the site, keeps ordinary access logs, such as your IP address and the page requested.
